After Years of No Results, This Man Found a Natural Approach That Got His Attention

By Sarah Mitchell | Health Correspondent  ·  Health Now

When Robert Jenkins, a 58-year-old truck driver from Columbus, Ohio, was diagnosed with type 2 diabetes in 2016, he found himself on multiple medications — yet his blood sugar remained difficult to manage for years.

"I felt incredibly frustrated," Robert told Health Now. "I was following my doctor's instructions, watching my diet, taking my prescriptions — but my A1C kept creeping up. I started wondering if I was missing something."

Everything changed when Robert's wife found an interview with James Harper, a 62-year-old former research fellow and independent wellness researcher from Portland, Oregon. After her own type 2 diagnosis in 2011, James Harper spent years studying peer-reviewed research on metabolic health and natural compounds that may support healthy glucose regulation.

What James Harper found about the role of inflammation in insulin resistance gave Robert a new perspective — and her simple daily protocol, using widely available ingredients, is now being evaluated by independent researchers. Over 40,000 people have explored her approach.

Robert Jenkins sits down with wellness researcher James Harper in an exclusive Health Now interview.

Sarah Mitchell: "Here at Health Now, we cover health stories that matter to everyday families. After Robert's story resonated with so many viewers asking 'Why didn't my doctor mention this?' — we reached out to James Harper for an in-depth conversation about what she's learned over fifteen years of independent research."

Robert Jenkins poses his first question to James Harper.

Sarah: "James Harper, your research has resonated with a lot of people. What was the turning point in your own health journey?"

James Harper explains the research behind her natural wellness protocol.

James Harper: "The turning point was understanding something that often gets overlooked: type 2 diabetes management isn't only about cutting sugar. There's growing research suggesting that chronic low-grade inflammation plays a meaningful role in how well the body responds to insulin over time.

Certain processed foods — ones that seem harmless — can contribute to an inflammatory environment that makes blood sugar harder to stabilize. I found this in peer-reviewed literature, and it helped me understand my own situation much better. When I shared this with Robert, it really clicked for him."

"The research on inflammation and insulin resistance opened up a whole new way of thinking about metabolic health — and it's backed by published studies."
— James Harper, Wellness Researcher

The two-hour interview covered diet, lifestyle factors, and the compounds James Harper incorporates into her daily protocol.

Sarah: "Your protocol uses natural compounds that address this. Can you explain how it works?"

James Harper walks through the key components of her research-backed protocol.

James Harper: "The protocol combines specific natural compounds that research suggests may help reduce inflammation, support insulin sensitivity, and promote healthy glucose metabolism. It works alongside — not in place of — medical guidance. Anyone on medication should always consult their doctor first.

For Robert personally, he began seeing improvements in his readings within several weeks of adopting the full protocol. His doctor was encouraged by the progress and they had a productive conversation about next steps. That said, everyone's body is different and results will vary from person to person."

Sarah Mitchell listens attentively as James Harper outlines the science behind her approach.

Sarah: "Is this something people with type 2 diabetes can explore safely at home?"

James Harper addresses viewer questions about safety and accessibility.

James Harper: "The ingredients are common, affordable, and available at most grocery stores — which is what makes this approach so accessible. That said, I always encourage people to keep their doctor informed about any lifestyle changes they make. I'm not a medical professional — I'm a researcher who found information that made a real difference in my own life and wanted to share it properly.

At 73, I use this protocol as part of my own wellness routine. I believe in it because I've lived it — and because the research behind it is solid and growing."
